## Direct generation prompt

```text
Create one continuous 8-second shot in 16:9.
Scene: A pale concrete doorway suspended above a misty alpine lake at dawn; ripples move gently across its reflection.
Creative purpose: Atmospheric opening for a short architecture film.
Composition and focus: Wide frame, doorway on the right third, clear reflected silhouette.
Light and palette: Pale lavender dawn with a warm edge on the concrete.
Camera: Slow push-in.
Timing: Open with the full doorway and reflection; drift slowly forward as mist passes; end with the doorway and reflection still aligned.
Continuity and exclusions: One continuous shot. Keep the doorway rigid, reflection coherent, and horizon stable. No titles or cuts.
Keep motion physically coherent and the subject stable between frames.
```

## Acceptance checks

- Deliver one video in the requested 16:9 framing, or disclose any supported substitute before rendering.

- Check opening, middle, and end for stable identity, plausible motion, and unintended cuts.

- Check that the result respects the stated exclusions and does not add unintended lettering.
